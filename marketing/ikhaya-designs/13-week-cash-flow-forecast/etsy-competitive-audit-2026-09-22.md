**Price: HOLD £9.99 base for now.**

The live UK-facing Etsy search for `small business cash flow spreadsheet 13 week forecast` returned a dense set of direct competitors. At the shop's current early-sales stage, £9.99 is commercially coherent with the owner's conversion-first strategy. There is no evidence-based reason in this search to reduce it again now. The next conversion opportunity is presentation, product proof and traffic.

## Commercial interpretation

### What buyers are being offered

The category has split into two broad propositions:

1. **Simple clarity:** a focused weekly cash view, warning signal and easy inputs, usually around £3–£14.
2. **Finance command centre:** AR/AP, scenarios, 12-month views, runway, dashboards and decision support, commonly around £16–£23 in this result set.

Ikhaya should not respond by adding features simply because competitors list more tabs. The product's commercial job is to make cash visibility feel manageable and useful. Feature inflation would conflict with the current instruction to sell and improve what already exists rather than rebuild it.

### Price conclusion

At **£9.99**, Ikhaya is:

- below the live result-set median;
- close enough to £7–£10 competitors to remain accessible;
- materially above the £2.83 bargain listing, avoiding an unnecessarily cheap signal;
- well below the £16–£23 premium cluster, appropriate while shop-level social proof is still developing.

**Recommendation: HOLD £9.99.** Revisit only after meaningful traffic/conversion evidence or a material change in product scope/social proof.

## Hero / search-thumbnail merchandising findings

The live results reinforce several recurring conversion patterns from the Christmas audit:

- buyers need immediate category recognition: `13 Week Cash Flow`, `Cash Flow Forecast` or equivalent should be unmistakable;
- the product itself needs to be visually credible, not hidden behind decorative lifestyle imagery;
- dashboard/forecast proof is a stronger trust device than generic finance decoration;
- short outcome language such as seeing a shortfall before it happens is more useful than a dense feature list in the hero;
- Excel / Google Sheets compatibility is repeatedly used as an immediate purchase-confidence cue;
- many listings compete by adding tabs and features, which creates an opening for Ikhaya to communicate calm professional clarity rather than visual complexity.

### Ikhaya hero classification

**PROVISIONAL: IMPROVE, not REBUILD.**

Reason: the current product proposition and £9.99 price are competitive. The likely conversion gain is making the existing real product proof dominant and immediately understandable at Etsy thumbnail size, while preserving Ikhaya's class/elegance/sophistication/organisation standard. A final KEEP / IMPROVE / REPLACE verdict requires direct inspection of the current rendered Ikhaya hero at search-thumbnail size. Do not make an external image change before that inspection.

If direct inspection confirms weak thumbnail comprehension, test a restrained hero with:
