At £21.59 the Ikhaya listing sits in the specialist middle-to-upper portion of the observed market rather than competing as a cheap spreadsheet.

The distribution job is therefore not to sell “an Excel spreadsheet”. It is to make the buyer understand the decision-support outcome: seeing the next 13 weeks of cash clearly enough to spot pressure early and manage runway with more confidence.

Do not claim capabilities that are not visible in the actual workbook or listing. Use real product screenshots for every demonstration-led Pin.

## Pinterest production rules

Current Pinterest Business guidance continues to recommend vertical creative, with 2:3 / 1000 × 1500 px as the standard image ratio, concise messaging, clear branding and a specific CTA. Pinterest also recommends creating original Pins regularly and attaching a destination URL so the Pin can drive traffic.

Use:
- 1000 × 1500 px static Pins as the default;
- actual dashboard/workbook screenshots, enlarged enough to be understood on mobile;
- one clear message per Pin;
- Ikhaya Designs GB branding that supports rather than dominates the product;
- a direct Etsy destination URL for the exact listing;
- specific, natural search language in titles and descriptions;
- no em dashes in customer-facing copy.

Do not:
- create generic “finance aesthetic” Pins with no product proof;
- lead with the cover or logo when the working dashboard is the reason to click;
- promise accounting, tax or financial advice;
- describe the spreadsheet as automated unless the actual workbook supports that claim;
- add invented results, savings or testimonials.

## QA record

### CREATE
Initial 8-Pin set + Reel produced around search, problem, outcome and product-proof angles.

### CRITIQUE
Sceptical buyer review identified four risks:
1. finance Pins can become generic business-content graphics with no proof of the actual product;
2. “runway” can sound sophisticated without showing what the buyer receives;
3. overclaiming automation/scenario features would weaken trust if not visibly supported by the workbook;
4. Pinterest traffic is commercially useless if Pins promise broader financial advice than the Etsy listing delivers.

### FIX
- made actual workbook screenshots mandatory for every product-led creative;
- removed unsupported automation/scenario language;
- made the exact Etsy product the destination and kept wording aligned to the live title;
- prioritised concrete 13-week visibility/runway outcomes over generic “manage your finances” language;
- created an educational Pin to attract search/discovery without drifting away from the paid tool;
