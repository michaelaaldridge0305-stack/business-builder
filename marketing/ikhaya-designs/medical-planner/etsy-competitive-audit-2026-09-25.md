## Competitive pattern

The search is heavily commoditised. Low prices are common and many listings are discounted. Stronger offers differentiate through breadth and specificity rather than simply calling themselves a “medical planner”: family health binder, chronic-illness planner, 50-page medical binder, Excel medical-record tracker, or a health CRM.

A current Etsy listing at £3.00 advertises 80+ pages and makes the contents explicit (appointments, medication, symptoms, blood pressure/blood sugar, allergies/vaccines, history, caregiver notes and emergency contacts). Another established printable bundle at £3.25 leads with the practical outcome “keep all of your medical information organised and easy to access” and visually shows real planner pages plus the included sizes.

## Merchandising lesson for Ikhaya

The hero must not rely on “Medical Planner” alone. At search-thumbnail size it should make the paid value visible immediately:

1. show **real planner pages** large enough to recognise;
2. communicate the practical outcome, e.g. keeping key health information together;
3. use one concise scope cue only if verified from the actual product;
4. preserve Ikhaya's calm, elegant green/neutral positioning rather than copying the category's generic clinical/minimalist aesthetic.

Do not cram a feature inventory into the hero. Use later listing images to prove breadth.
