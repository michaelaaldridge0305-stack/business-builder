## Merchandising / hero findings

Direct guide heroes use simple category recognition rather than elaborate lifestyle storytelling. The stronger commercial lesson is not to imitate their aesthetics but to make the product type and beginner payoff instantly legible.

For Ikhaya, the hero should communicate in one glance:

1. this is a practical beginner Notion guide;
2. it reduces overwhelm / confusion;
3. the buyer will learn enough to use Notion confidently;
4. the visual feels classier and calmer than the noisy template market.

Avoid presenting it like a generic ebook cover with no proof of what is inside. Later images should show genuine interior pages, the learning path, specific topics covered, and who it is for.

## Commercial assessment

### Evidence-supported conclusions

- Direct low/mid-tier guide competition is cheap.
- Trust changes the comparison: the £8.05 guide has 26 ratings, while the £1.08 and £2.83 direct guides show no ratings.
- Ready-made Notion templates create severe substitute pressure at roughly £2–£9 and often carry much stronger review counts.
- Therefore a new/low-review Ikhaya beginner guide needs a very clear reason to buy a guide instead of either free Notion help or a ready-made template.

### Positioning recommendation

Do not sell `information about Notion`. Sell the transformation from `Notion feels overwhelming` to `I understand what I am doing and can build/use it calmly`.

Keep Ikhaya's class/elegance/sophistication/organisation standard. Use real interior proof and a simple learning-path visual. Do not compete by stuffing the listing with hundreds of templates or by copying discount-heavy marketplace styling.
