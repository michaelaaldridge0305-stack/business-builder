This reinforces that Ikhaya cannot depend on shop trust alone at its current early-sales stage; the hero must communicate value immediately.

## What is winning the first-click battle

Across the strongest relevant search imagery, five patterns recur:

1. **The working product is large.** The spreadsheet/dashboard is normally the visual centre, not a small prop.
2. **The category is obvious.** Buyers can identify Christmas + planner/tracker + spreadsheet before opening the listing.
3. **Copy is short.** Strong heroes tend to use one direct headline plus, at most, a format or feature cue.
4. **Proof beats decoration.** Product screenshots carry more commercial information than generic presents, baubles or lifestyle props.
5. **Format reassurance matters.** Google Sheets is often surfaced visually because it answers a purchase-risk question immediately.

## Competitive weakness Ikhaya should exploit

Much of the category is visually noisy: bright red/green, pink, badges, bows, clip-art Christmas motifs, multiple feature pills and crowded spreadsheet screenshots. This creates a useful opening for Ikhaya.

The opportunity is **not** to become more festive. It is to become more immediately legible while looking calmer, more elegant and more organised than the surrounding results.

## Hero decision

**Classification: IMPROVE, not rebuild.**

The product proposition is strong enough and the recent calm Christmas direction should be retained. The hero should only be replaced if the live listing image cannot meet the specification below at Etsy thumbnail size.

The required conversion move is to make the real Dashboard substantially larger and reduce anything that competes with it.

## QA-passed hero specification

### Primary message

**Christmas Gift Organiser**

### Secondary cue

**Google Sheets | Gifts, Budget & Wrapping**

This is intentionally shorter than the Pinterest headline. Etsy search imagery has less time and less space. The product screenshot should prove the detail.

### Composition

- Keep the established warm cream background and refined Ikhaya visual language.
- Make the **real Dashboard the dominant object**, targeting roughly 60–70% of the meaningful image area.
- Dashboard must be large enough that charts/sections visibly read as a functioning organiser even when cell-level text is not readable.
- Use forest green as the primary brand anchor with restrained cranberry/burgundy as an accent only.
- If botanical/seasonal detail is used, keep it peripheral and sparse. It must frame rather than decorate the product.
- Avoid baubles, candy canes, piles of gifts, snowmen, novelty Christmas fonts, large badges and feature-pill clutter.
- Do not add multiple small spreadsheet screenshots to hero image 1. The six-tab system belongs in subsequent listing images.
- Do not put price on the artwork.
- Do not use fake spreadsheet UI.
- Keep the actual Ikhaya Designs GB brand treatment subtle enough that it does not compete with product/category recognition.

### Search-thumbnail test

At small mobile/search size, a buyer must be able to answer within roughly one glance:

1. This is a Christmas organisation product.
2. It is a digital spreadsheet / Google Sheets tool.
3. It helps with gifts and budget, not merely a printable list.
4. It looks calmer and more premium than the surrounding festive templates.

If any of these fail, the hero is not ready.

## Image-set implication

Do not force the hero to explain all six tabs. Use the rest of the Etsy image set to progressively sell the system:

1. Hero: product/category + large Dashboard proof.
2. Six-tab system: show the full organiser architecture.
3. Gift Tracker: buying / delivery / wrapping workflow.
4. Budget: planned versus actual spend.
5. People & Ideas: capture ideas, interests, sizes and links.
6. Don't Forget: last-minute / easily missed jobs.
7. How it works / access: Google Sheets and access process.

This preserves a clean hero while still making the listing comprehensive.

## CREATE -> CRITIQUE -> FIX -> RE-CHECK -> PASS

### CREATE
Initial recommendation favoured a large Dashboard, minimal headline and restrained Christmas styling.

### CRITIQUE
- **Buyer:** A purely aesthetic cream hero could become too subtle and fail to communicate Christmas/category quickly.
- **Commercial:** Too much elegance without format proof could lose clicks to louder competitors that immediately show Google Sheets.
- **Brand:** Copying bright competitor conventions would undermine Ikhaya's class/elegance/sophistication positioning.
- **Accuracy:** Hero cannot imply features that are not present or use recreated spreadsheet UI.

### FIX
- Added explicit `Google Sheets | Gifts, Budget & Wrapping` cue.
- Increased real Dashboard target to 60–70% of meaningful area.
- Limited hero to one dominant real-product proof rather than trying to show all tabs.
- Kept Christmas recognition through restrained cues and naming, not festive clutter.
- Moved feature depth into the subsequent image sequence.

### RE-CHECK
- Immediate product comprehension: PASS
- Search-thumbnail hierarchy: PASS at specification level
- Competitive differentiation: PASS
- Ikhaya brand fit: PASS
- Product accuracy: PASS
- Avoids unnecessary rebuilding: PASS

## Decision

**Proceed with an IMPROVE route.** Do not commission a wholesale visual rebuild. The highest-value change is a larger real Dashboard plus shorter category/format messaging. If the current hero already meets this specification when rendered at Etsy thumbnail size, classify it KEEP and make no change.
