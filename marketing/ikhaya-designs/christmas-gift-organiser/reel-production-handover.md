# Christmas Gift Organiser Reel Production Handover

**Business:** Ikhaya Designs GB  
**Product:** Christmas Gift Organiser  
**Prepared:** 14 September 2026  
**Status:** INTERNAL CREATIVE + QA COMPLETE; MANUAL CANVA ASSEMBLY REQUIRED

## Commercial objective

Create a short vertical Reel/Pinterest video that shows the emotional outcome of the Christmas Gift Organiser: Christmas admin has been handled, so the customer can relax.

This is a conversion/distribution asset for the existing product. It is not a product rebuild and must not become a generic Christmas lifestyle video with no product proof.

## New explicit owner direction incorporated

Recent production testing established four non-negotiables:

1. The woman must remain clearly visible because her relaxed state is the meaning of the scenario.
2. Landscape footage must not be centre-cropped into 9:16 when that crops the woman out.
3. Shrinking landscape footage inside a blurred 9:16 background is rejected because the meaningful footage becomes too small.
4. Free AI-video tools/credit-limited generators are not a dependable production route. Use a native 9:16 lifestyle still with subtle Canva movement instead of forcing unsuitable landscape video.

## Approved production route

Build the Reel from a **native 1080 x 1920 lifestyle still** plus real product screenshots and light Canva movement.

Do not use landscape footage as the hero visual.

### Scene 1: calm outcome

**Duration:** 0 to 5 seconds  
**Visual:** Native 9:16 warm Christmas living-room scene. Woman relaxing on sofa with drink; Christmas tree remains visible. Calm cream, neutral, muted green and warm-light aesthetic.  
**Motion:** Very slow pan/zoom only. No frantic animation.

**Text sequence:**

- `The presents are sorted.`
- `The budget is under control.`
- `Nothing left to remember.`

Text must be added in Canva as editable text, not generated inside an image.

### Scene 2: product proof

**Duration:** 5 to 8 seconds  
**Visual:** Actual Christmas Gift Organiser dashboard displayed clearly on a laptop/tablet or clean device mock-up. The spreadsheet must be the real product, not an invented AI interface.

**Primary text:**

`Christmas planning, without the chaos.`

**Secondary text:**

`Christmas Gift Organiser`

Do not add unsupported product claims.

### Scene 3: closing outcome / CTA

**Duration:** 8 to 11 seconds  
**Visual:** Calm festive background plus recognisable product proof, using the same visual language as Scenes 1 and 2.

**Text:**

`Now you can actually enjoy Christmas.`

`Ikhaya Designs GB`

`Available on Etsy`

The Reel may imply calm and organisation but must not imply automated purchasing, reminders, AI, or functionality not present in the product.

## Visual rules

- Native vertical composition: 1080 x 1920.
- Woman and tree both remain visible in the lifestyle scene.
- Product screenshot must be large enough to recognise on a phone.
- Do not dump a raw spreadsheet screenshot onto the page without considered framing.
- Keep the aesthetic calm, premium and practical rather than dark, aggressive or heavily red/green.
- No pink unless deliberately re-approved.
- Use the correct Ikhaya Designs GB identity.
- Avoid invented slogans.
- Do not use AI-generated visible text inside lifestyle imagery.

## Text regression QA

The owner previously deleted a Reel because generated text contained corrupted spaces/stray `n` characters. A later Pin also contained a spelling error. These are mandatory regression checks.

Before PASS, inspect every visible character at 100% zoom for:

- missing spaces;
- stray `n` or other inserted letters;
- misspellings;
- malformed punctuation;
- broken apostrophes;
- duplicated words;
- AI artefacts embedded in background props/books/screens;
- clipped text or text hidden by UI safe areas.

Any visible AI-generated wording on props must be removed or obscured if it could be read as Ikhaya copy.

## Minimal manual Canva assembly

1. Open a new Instagram Reel / mobile-video design at 1080 x 1920.
2. Add the approved native 9:16 lifestyle still as Scene 1.
3. Set Scene 1 to ~5 seconds and apply a slow, subtle photo movement.
4. Add the three exact Scene 1 text lines as editable Canva text.
5. Add Scene 2 with a real dashboard screenshot on a clean device/product-proof layout; set to ~3 seconds.
6. Add the exact Scene 2 copy.
7. Add Scene 3; set to ~3 seconds and add the exact closing copy.
8. Use restrained transitions only.
9. Run the mandatory character-by-character text regression QA at full size and mobile preview.
10. Export as 1080 x 1920 MP4 only after PASS.
