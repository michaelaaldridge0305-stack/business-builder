## 2. Current product proposition to carry through every traffic asset

The product is not simply a Christmas budget spreadsheet.

It is a six-tab Google Sheets system:

1. Dashboard
2. People & Ideas
3. Gift Tracker
4. Budget
5. Don't Forget
6. Start Here

It helps the buyer:

- set an overall Christmas gift budget;
- plan spending for each recipient;
- capture gift ideas, interests, sizes and things people mention;
- save product/shop links;
- compare planned and actual spend;
- track buying, delivery, wrapping, posting and giving;
- see remaining budget and outstanding jobs at a glance;
- remember easy-to-miss extras such as teacher gifts, Secret Santa, cards, batteries and postage.

### Core traffic promise

**Keep every Christmas present, budget and last-minute job in one calm place.**

### Differentiation to demonstrate visually

The paid value is the connected system, particularly:

- ideas captured before they are forgotten;
- per-person planning;
- gift-status tracking beyond “bought/not bought”;
- a live dashboard;
- budget visibility;
- a dedicated “Don't Forget” layer for small jobs that create Christmas stress.

Traffic creative should show these working parts rather than leading with a decorative cover.
