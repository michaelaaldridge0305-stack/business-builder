# Christmas Gift Organiser — Live Etsy Price Benchmark

**Date:** 18 September 2026
**Market:** Etsy, UK buyer view, GBP
**Workstream:** Existing-product conversion

## Owner direction incorporated

Michaela's current commercial priority is sales volume and buyer trust before margin maximisation. The shop currently has 7 sales. The Christmas Gift Organiser has already been moved to a £1.99 base price, with a recently observed UK buyer-visible price of approximately £2.39. Do not change pricing again without a new evidence-based reason and the applicable owner approval.

## Live search evidence

A live Etsy search for `Christmas gift planner spreadsheet gift tracker budget Google Sheets` returned 20 directly relevant spreadsheet/planner listings. Observed buyer-visible prices were:

£0.93, £2.53, £1.12, £5.07, £3.75, £2.11, £11.26, £1.62, £4.49, £2.53, £6.01, £1.01, £4.20, £5.62, £5.62, £0.93, £9.35, £13.13, £1.96, £3.71.

Calculated benchmark:
- **Median:** £3.73
- **Lowest:** £0.93
- **Highest:** £13.13
- **9 of 20** results were below £3
- Heavy discounting was common in the returned set, so buyer-visible sale price matters more than nominal list price for search-result competitiveness.

## Commercial assessment

At approximately £2.39 buyer-visible, Ikhaya is already below the £3.73 observed median and inside the dense low-price competitive band. That supports the owner's volume-first strategy without requiring another immediate Christmas price cut.

The evidence does **not** support racing to the absolute floor (£0.93–£1.12). With only seven shop sales, reducing purchase friction is sensible, but Ikhaya still needs enough perceived value to avoid looking disposable. The current £1.99 base price is a reasonable test point.

## Conversion implication

For Christmas, the higher-value GREEN work is now merchandising and distribution, not further price reduction. The next conversion question is whether the hero image can outperform £1–£3 competitors at search-thumbnail size and communicate that this is a six-tab system rather than a basic gift list.

## Decision

**PASS — HOLD CHRISTMAS PRICE FOR TEST.**

Keep £1.99 base price for the current test window. Do not recommend a further reduction until traffic produces enough impressions/clicks to distinguish a price problem from a thumbnail/listing problem.

## Next measurement

Track qualified Pinterest outbound clicks, Etsy visits, favourites and orders after first-wave distribution. If there is meaningful listing traffic but weak purchase conversion, revisit price alongside listing trust/ease. If impressions exist but clicks remain weak, prioritise hero/Pin creative rather than price.
