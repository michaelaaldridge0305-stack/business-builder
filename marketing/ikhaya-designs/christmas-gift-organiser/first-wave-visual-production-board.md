## Shared visual rules

- Use real product screenshots. Never generate a fake spreadsheet interface.
- Keep the Ikhaya calm, practical aesthetic. No dark/aggressive backgrounds.
- Do not add pink.
- No invented slogans or unsupported feature claims.
- No em dashes in customer-facing copy.
- Do not place price on evergreen traffic creative.
- Keep text editable in Canva. Do not bake generated wording into AI imagery.
- Decorative Christmas styling must support, not obscure, product proof.
- Check every asset at full size and at a phone-sized thumbnail before PASS.

## Mandatory text regression check

Before any asset is approved or published, inspect every rendered character manually for:

- `n` or another stray character where a space should be;
- missing or doubled spaces;
- misspellings;
- malformed apostrophes;
- accidental punctuation;
- clipped words;
- unexpected line breaks;
- AI-generated gibberish inside imagery;
- inconsistent capitalisation.

Known regression incidents: corrupted spaces in a prior Reel and `DISTILL` misspelling in a prior Pin. These are release-blocking defects.
