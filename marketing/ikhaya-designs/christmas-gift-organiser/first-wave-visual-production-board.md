## Purpose

Turn the approved distribution strategy into a production-level visual board for the first four Pinterest assets without rebuilding the product. Each concept uses real product evidence and must earn a click while preserving Ikhaya's refined brand position.

## 19 September owner direction

Ikhaya Designs GB should communicate **class, elegance, sophistication and organisation**. Christmas assets must still look unmistakably Ikhaya rather than like generic seasonal Etsy graphics. Avoid excessive red/green decoration, baubles, candy canes, clutter and tacky festive motifs. Use restrained seasonal cues within the established cream, forest-green, burgundy/neutral visual language. Competitors may inform hierarchy and conversion technique, but not the aesthetic identity.

The current commercial phase also prioritises accessible pricing and sales volume/trust-building. Do not put price on Pinterest artwork because prices may be adjusted after Etsy benchmarking.

## Live Canva evidence checked

**Decision:** reuse components only where they improve speed and still meet the current visual standard. Do not preserve generic messaging or festive styling merely because it already exists.

## Shared visual rules

- Brand test: class, elegance, sophistication and organisation.
- Use real product screenshots. Never generate a fake spreadsheet interface.
- Keep the Ikhaya calm, practical aesthetic.
- Use restrained Christmas cues; avoid generic festive clutter, baubles, candy-cane styling and excessive red/green.
- Do not add pink.
- No invented slogans or unsupported feature claims.
- No em dashes in customer-facing copy.
- Do not place price on traffic creative.
- Keep text editable in Canva. Do not bake generated wording into AI imagery.
- Decorative styling must support, not obscure, product proof.
- Check every asset at full size and at a phone-sized thumbnail before PASS.

## Mandatory text regression check

Before any asset is approved or published, inspect every rendered character manually for stray characters where spaces should be, missing/doubled spaces, misspellings, malformed apostrophes, accidental punctuation, clipped words, unexpected line breaks, AI-generated gibberish and inconsistent capitalisation.

Known regression incidents: corrupted spaces in a prior Reel and `DISTILL` misspelling in a prior Pin. These are release-blocking defects.
