## Short listing description

You already have the numbers. The difficult part is turning them into management commentary without spending ages writing it or letting AI confidently invent an explanation that the accounts do not support.

Numbers to Management Narrative gives finance professionals a repeatable way to use an AI assistant for the drafting stage while keeping evidence, judgement and sign-off under human control.

It includes structured input templates, a reusable finance-specific AI instruction, house-style controls, worked examples of plausible AI mistakes, a final verification checklist and practical troubleshooting.

Built for finance professionals preparing management accounts. No special software, calls or bespoke implementation required.

## Who it is for

Finance professionals who already prepare management accounts and want to reduce the writing and checking burden of turning validated figures and known business context into concise management commentary.

The user should already be able to validate their accounts and exercise professional judgement. The toolkit helps with drafting and quality control, not with doing the accounting for them.

## Who it is not for

Not suitable if you want AI to calculate or reconcile your accounts, perform the underlying variance investigation, make accounting judgements for you, or provide professional sign-off.

It is also not a done-for-you service and does not include calls, review of your management accounts or software implementation.

## Key proposition

**Let AI help write the commentary. Do not let it invent the story.**

The toolkit is designed around a common AI failure in finance: a plausible sentence can sound professional while being more certain than the evidence allows. The workflow forces a separation between validated figures, known facts, assumptions and unresolved unknowns before the narrative is drafted.

## FAQ

### Does this calculate my management accounts?

No. Start with figures you have already validated. The toolkit helps with the narrative and verification stage.

### Which AI tool do I need?

The method is written for a general-purpose AI assistant capable of following detailed text instructions. The product does not certify or require a particular provider. Use only a service approved by your organisation for the information you intend to provide.

### Can I paste confidential company accounts into AI?

Only if your organisation has approved the AI service and that use of the data. If you are unsure, anonymise or fictionalise the information and remove identifiers. The toolkit does not override your employer's security, privacy or AI-use policies.

### Will the AI write the final commentary for me?

It can produce a first draft, but you remain responsible for checking the numbers, evidence, wording, accounting judgement and final sign-off.

### Is this accounting advice?

No. It is a self-service drafting and quality-control toolkit for finance professionals.

### Do I get support?

The product includes troubleshooting for the workflow itself. It does not include calls, review of your real accounts, bespoke prompt engineering or accounting advice.

## Launch announcement

**Numbers to Management Narrative is built for a very specific finance problem: the numbers are finished, but the management commentary still needs writing.**

AI can make that stage quicker to draft, but fluent output creates its own risk. A model can turn a partial explanation into a complete one, treat an indicator as a confirmed outcome, or invent a perfectly plausible cause that nobody actually supplied.

This toolkit gives finance professionals a structured way to use AI without handing over professional judgement. You organise the validated numbers and evidence, AI drafts the narrative, and a finance-specific verification process helps you challenge it before sign-off.

It is deliberately small and practical rather than an AI course: reusable templates, one core instruction, worked failure examples, house-style controls and a final checklist.
