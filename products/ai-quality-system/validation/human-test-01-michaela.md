## Scenario

You are Finance Manager of **Harbour & Field Ltd**, a fictional UK company supplying commercial landscaping equipment to trade customers. You are preparing August management commentary for the Managing Director.

The MD wants a short explanation of the month: what materially changed, what is known, what remains uncertain, what needs management attention and what should be checked before sign-off.

All figures below are validated source data. The task is not to recalculate the accounts. It is to use AI to help interpret and communicate them.

### Validated contextual facts

1. A customer order worth **£92k** that was expected to ship in August was delayed by the customer and shipped on 3 September. The August budget included this order. No evidence has been provided that the order was cancelled.
2. August gross margin benefited from a supplier rebate. Finance estimates approximately **£11k** of rebate income is included in August gross profit. The rebate was budgeted evenly across the year rather than specifically in August.
3. A new sales manager joined in July. Their August employment cost was approximately **£6k**. No complete payroll variance analysis has yet been performed.
4. Marketing includes **£14k** for a trade exhibition held in August. The annual marketing budget included £10k for the exhibition, with the remaining £4k overspend not yet analysed.
5. Inventory includes approximately **£160k** of a new mower range purchased ahead of its September launch. Management deliberately approved the stock build.
6. Separately, **£85k** of older accessories has had no sale in the last 120 days. Operations is reviewing whether this reflects seasonality, excess stock or products requiring a markdown. No impairment decision has been made.
7. No evidence has been provided of a general supplier price reduction, permanent improvement in product mix, broad customer discounting, cancelled sales orders, or an inventory write-off.
8. The September forecast has not yet been refreshed for the delayed £92k order or the stock position.

## Your task

Use an AI assistant to help you prepare sign-off-ready August management commentary for the MD.

Your final output should contain:

- a short executive summary;
- 3 to 5 priority commentary points;
- a short questions/actions-before-sign-off section.

Aim for something you would genuinely be comfortable using as the basis of management commentary, not an academic answer.

## Quality controls to use with your AI assistant

You may phrase these naturally rather than copying them word for word. Before accepting the AI output, make sure it has considered:

1. whether figures, percentages, periods and favourable/adverse directions match the supplied information;
2. whether explanations are no stronger than the evidence supporting them;
3. whether known factors explain a whole movement or only part of it;
4. whether timing, phasing, classification or one-off effects change how a movement should be interpreted;
5. whether the commentary focuses on matters that could affect a management decision;
6. whether risks are described precisely without turning an indicator into a confirmed outcome;
7. whether the AI has introduced a cause, trend or business fact that was not supplied;
8. whether uncertainty has been handled without burying clear conclusions in caveats;
9. whether material unknowns have a useful follow-up action.

The AI is preparing decision support. **You remain responsible for final judgement and sign-off.**

## What to record during the test

Please use a timer. Record:

- **Build time:** from starting the AI task until you have its first complete draft.
- **Review/correction time:** from receiving that draft until you are personally comfortable with the final commentary.
- **Total time.**

Also keep the **first complete AI draft** as well as your **final corrected version**. Do not tidy the first draft retrospectively.

After finishing, record these four quick ratings from 1 to 5:

- confidence in the final commentary;
- how easy the method was to apply;
- how much the quality controls helped;
- likelihood you would use this approach again next month.

Finally answer, without being shown any proposed price first:

1. What was the most useful part of the method?
2. What felt unnecessary or cumbersome?
3. What, if anything, did the AI get wrong or overstate?
4. Did the checks save you effort overall, or create extra work?
5. If this were a polished reusable toolkit with worked finance examples, templates and job-specific checks, would you personally regard it as worth paying for? If yes, what price would feel reasonable? If no, why not?
