# Human Transfer Test 02 - Participant Pack

**Status:** Ready for participant  
**Purpose:** Test whether the revised management-commentary controls transfer to a new finance scenario.

## Test integrity

Use only this participant pack during the exercise. Do not open evaluator notes, expected findings or earlier test answers until the first draft, final version, timings and ratings have been recorded.

All company information is fictional.

## Interruption rule

Run separate timers for build and review.

If interrupted:

1. pause the timer immediately;
2. record the interruption;
3. resume only when work restarts;
4. if the timer was not paused, mark that timing as invalid rather than estimating it.

## Scenario

You are Finance Manager of **Meridian Office Interiors Ltd**, a fictional UK business supplying and installing office furniture. You are preparing September management commentary for the Managing Director.

The MD needs a concise explanation of what materially changed, what is known, what remains uncertain and what requires action.

All figures are validated source data. AI is assisting with interpretation and drafting, not recalculating the accounts.

### September management data - £000 unless stated

| Metric | September Actual | September Budget | September LY | YTD Actual | YTD Budget |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 1,420 | 1,300 | 1,255 | 10,210 | 10,080 |
| Gross profit | 398 | 390 | 377 | 2,933 | 3,024 |
| Gross margin | 28.0% | 30.0% | 30.0% | 28.7% | 30.0% |
| Installation payroll | 214 | 185 | 176 | 1,518 | 1,402 |
| Subcontractors | 96 | 72 | 68 | 604 | 545 |
| Other operating costs | 61 | 64 | 60 | 451 | 458 |
| Operating profit | 27 | 69 | 73 | 360 | 619 |
| Closing receivables | 1,860 | 1,520 | 1,410 | - | - |
| Debtor days | 49 | 41 | 40 | - | - |

### Validated contextual facts

1. September revenue includes a £210k project originally expected in October. Installation was completed and accepted by the customer in September. The project remains in the latest full-year forecast, so this is a timing movement rather than additional forecast revenue.
2. The £210k project used a larger subcontract installation team because the customer brought the completion date forward. Its project gross margin has not yet been calculated separately.
3. Installation payroll includes £18k of approved overtime connected with three projects. Finance has not completed analysis of the remaining monthly or year-to-date payroll variance.
4. Subcontractor costs include £31k associated with the accelerated project. No complete subcontractor variance analysis is available.
5. A £240k customer balance became overdue on 26 September. The customer has disputed £35k relating to installation changes but has not disputed the remaining balance. No payment date is confirmed.
6. No bad-debt provision or write-off decision has been made.
7. No evidence has been provided of a general selling-price reduction, broad discounting, permanent labour inefficiency or supplier cost inflation.
8. The October monthly forecast has not been rephased for the accelerated project.

## Task

Use an AI assistant to prepare sign-off-ready September management commentary for the MD.

Include:

- a short executive summary;
- three to five priority commentary points;
- a short questions/actions-before-sign-off section.

## Quality controls

Before accepting the output, make sure the AI has considered:

1. numerical fidelity, units, periods and variance direction;
2. comparator clarity;
3. whether each explanation is supported by evidence;
4. whether known factors explain the whole movement or only part;
5. timing and phasing versus underlying or full-year performance;
6. materiality and decision relevance;
7. precise risk language;
8. unsupported causes or business facts;
9. usefulness without excessive caution;
10. proportionate actions for material unknowns.

You remain responsible for final judgement and sign-off.

## What to record

Keep:

- the first complete AI draft;
- the final corrected version;
- build time;
- valid active review/correction time;
- interruptions;
- total valid active time.

Rate from 1 to 5:

- confidence in the final commentary;
- ease of applying the method;
- helpfulness of the quality controls;
- likelihood of using the approach again.

Answer:

1. What was most useful?
2. What felt unnecessary or cumbersome?
3. What did the AI get wrong or overstate?
4. Did the checks save effort overall?
5. Would a polished reusable toolkit be worth paying for? If yes, at what price?
