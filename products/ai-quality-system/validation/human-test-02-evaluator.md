# Human Transfer Test 02 - Evaluator Pack

**Confidentiality:** Do not show this file to the participant before the test record is complete.

## Evaluation purpose

Assess technical quality, correction burden, transferability and professional usefulness. Do not reward the output merely for repeating the supplied controls.

## Expected analytical findings

A strong output should recognise that:

- September revenue is £120k above budget, but the £210k October project was brought forward, so the result is not evidence of additional full-year revenue.
- Excluding the timing movement mechanically would leave September revenue below budget, but the output must not invent a definitive adjusted performance measure without considering the project's other effects.
- Gross margin is 28.0% compared with 30.0% budget and last year.
- The accelerated project may contribute to the margin movement, but its separate margin is unknown, so it cannot explain the full decline as fact.
- Operating profit is £42k below budget despite revenue being above budget.
- Approved overtime explains £18k of the £29k monthly installation-payroll variance, leaving at least £11k requiring analysis. It does not explain the full £116k year-to-date variance.
- The £31k accelerated-project subcontract cost is material, but does not by itself explain the £24k monthly variance because the budget treatment and other movements are not supplied.
- Receivables and debtor days have worsened.
- Only £35k of the £240k overdue balance is stated to be disputed. The whole balance must not be described as disputed.
- The overdue balance is a collection and credit-risk indicator, not a confirmed bad debt.
- October should be rephased for the accelerated project without implying a full-year forecast change.

## Hidden traps

Flag if the output:

- presents the revenue beat as underlying growth;
- treats the £210k as incremental full-year revenue;
- attributes all margin decline to subcontractors or the accelerated project;
- treats £18k overtime as explaining all payroll variance;
- treats £31k subcontractor cost as a straightforward explanation of the entire £24k adverse variance without addressing budget treatment;
- says the full £240k balance is disputed;
- presents the overdue amount as a confirmed bad debt;
- explains weak profit through weak revenue;
- confuses September, year-to-date and full-year implications;
- becomes so cautious that it fails to state clear supported conclusions.

## Scoring

Score 0 to 2 on each dimension:

|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Material error | Minor/mixed | Strong |
| Analytical validity | Material leap | Mixed | Conclusions follow |
| Explanatory calibration | Over/understated | Mixed | Evidence matched |
| Materiality | Poor focus | Acceptable | Strong priorities |
| Management usefulness | Low | Some | Decision-useful |
| Clarity and brevity | Poor | Acceptable | Strong |
| Risk judgement | Misstated | Mixed | Precise |
| Action quality | Weak | Generic | Specific |
| Traceability | Difficult | Mixed | Easy |
| Human verification effort | Rebuild | Repairable | Efficient |

Maximum score: 20.

## Critical failures

Record separately:

- invented figure;
- material sign, direction or comparator error;
- unsupported cause stated as fact;
- timing movement presented as underlying or incremental performance;
- risk indicator escalated into a confirmed outcome;
- output too hedged to communicate clear conclusions;
- AI presented as owner of final sign-off.

## Correction burden

Record:

- material claims;
- factual or analytical corrections;
- house-style corrections;
- traceback friction: Low, Medium or High;
- valid active correction time;
- discard/rebuild: Yes or No.

## Decision rules

- **Advance:** no critical failure, low factual correction burden, net time saving and professional usability.
- **Modify:** useful but correction burden, length, clarity or control design requires another iteration.
- **Stop:** critical reliability problem, no net time saving or no credible paid value.
