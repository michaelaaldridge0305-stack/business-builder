### Executive summary

August operating profit was £25k, £36k below budget and £17k below last year. Revenue was £70k below budget, although £55k higher than last year. ### Priority commentary

1. **Revenue**

   August revenue of £980k was £70k below budget but £55k above last year. The budget included a £92k customer order which was delayed at the customer’s request and shipped on 3 September. This more than explains the headline shortfall against budget. The order has not been cancelled, but the September forecast needs updating to reflect its revised timing.

2. **Gross profit and margin**

   Gross profit was £310k, £16k below budget, while gross margin of 31.6% was 0.6 percentage points above budget and broadly consistent with last year. August gross profit included approximately £11k of supplier rebate income. As the rebate was spread evenly in the budget, the reported margin should not be treated as evidence of a permanent improvement in product mix or supplier pricing. The underlying margin position should be confirmed before sign-off.

3. **Operating costs and profit**

   Operating profit was £25k, £36k below budget. Payroll was £6k over budget for the month and £36k over budget year to date. The new sales manager’s August employment cost was approximately £6k, but a complete payroll variance analysis has not yet been performed, so this should not be assumed to explain the full year-to-date variance.

   Marketing was £17k above the August budget. The £14k trade exhibition was included in the annual plan at £10k, accounting for £4k of overspend, but the remaining monthly variance requires further analysis. Other operating costs were £3k below budget.

4. **Inventory**

   Closing inventory was £1.18m, £250k above budget, with inventory days at 78 compared with 61 budgeted. Approximately £160k relates to the deliberately approved stock build for the September mower launch, explaining part but not all of the increase. Separately, £85k of older accessories has recorded no sales for 120 days. This is a risk indicator rather than evidence that a write-off is required. Operations should complete its review of seasonality, excess stock and potential markdown requirements.

5. **Year-to-date position**

   Year-to-date revenue was £80k below budget and operating profit was £30k below budget. Gross profit was nevertheless £19k ahead of budget, supported by a gross margin 0.5 percentage points above budget. Marketing and payroll were respectively £27k and £36k over budget, partly offset by a £14k favourable variance in other operating costs.

### Questions and actions before sign-off

- Refresh the September forecast for the £92k order that shipped on 3 September and consider the related profit and inventory timing.
- Complete the payroll variance analysis, particularly the £36k year-to-date overspend.
- Reconcile the full £17k August marketing variance, including the exhibition overspend and budget phasing.
- Confirm the accounting and phasing of the approximately £11k supplier rebate and assess the underlying gross margin.
- Complete the review of the £85k slow-moving accessories and document whether any markdown or impairment is required.
- Explain the remaining inventory increase after allowing for the approved £160k mower stock build.
