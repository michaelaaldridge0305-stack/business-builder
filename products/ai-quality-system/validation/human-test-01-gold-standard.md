# Human Transfer Test 01 - Gold-Standard Commentary

**Status:** Post-test reference version  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate the intended balance of accuracy, brevity, professional British tone and useful action.

## Executive summary

August operating profit was £25k, £36k below budget and £17k below last year. Revenue was £70k below budget, although £55k ahead of last year. The delayed £92k order more than explains the monthly revenue shortfall and shipped on 3 September, so this is a timing issue rather than evidence of lost demand. Gross margin was ahead of budget but included approximately £11k of supplier rebate income. Payroll, marketing and inventory require further review before sign-off.

## Priority commentary

1. **Revenue and timing**

   Revenue was £980k, £70k below budget and £55k ahead of last year. The August budget included a £92k order that was delayed at the customer's request and shipped on 3 September. Rephase the order into September, but do not treat it as additional full-year revenue.

2. **Gross profit and margin**

   Gross profit was £310k, £16k below budget. Gross margin was 31.6% compared with 31.0% budget and 31.5% last year. Approximately £11k of supplier rebate income benefited August, so the reported margin does not establish a permanent improvement in product mix or supplier pricing. Confirm the rebate treatment and assess the underlying margin before sign-off.

3. **Operating costs and profit**

   Operating profit was £25k, £36k below budget. Payroll was £6k above budget in August and £36k above budget year to date. The new sales manager explains approximately £6k of the August cost, but the full monthly and year-to-date variances have not been analysed.

   Marketing was £17k above budget in August. The exhibition cost £14k against £10k in the annual plan, explaining £4k of overspend. The remaining £13k monthly variance requires analysis. Other operating costs were £3k below budget.

4. **Inventory**

   Closing inventory was £1.18m, £250k above budget. Inventory days increased to 78, compared with 61 budgeted and 59 last year. The approved £160k mower stock build explains part of the increase, leaving a material balance to investigate. The separate £85k of accessories with no sales for 120 days is a slow-moving-stock risk, not evidence that an impairment is required. Complete the review before deciding whether any markdown or impairment is appropriate.

5. **Year-to-date position**

   Year-to-date revenue was £80k below budget and operating profit was £30k below budget. Gross profit remained £19k ahead of budget, with gross margin at 31.5% compared with 31.0% budget. Payroll and marketing were £36k and £27k above budget respectively, partly offset by other operating costs being £14k below budget.

## Actions before sign-off

- Rephase the £92k order into September and confirm whether the monthly movement changes any operational assumptions, without altering the full-year revenue outlook unless new evidence supports it.
- Complete the monthly and year-to-date payroll variance analysis.
- Reconcile the remaining £13k August marketing variance and confirm budget phasing.
- Confirm the supplier rebate accounting treatment and quantify the underlying gross margin.
- Reconcile the inventory increase after the approved mower stock build.
- Complete the slow-moving-accessories review and document the basis for any markdown or impairment decision.

**Human decision:** The Finance Manager reviews, amends where necessary and approves the final commentary.
