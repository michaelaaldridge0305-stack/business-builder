## Problem section

### The numbers can be finished while the commentary is still sitting on your to-do list

AI can produce management commentary quickly, but fluent wording can hide weak reasoning. A model can turn a partial explanation into a complete one, treat a risk indicator as a confirmed outcome, or invent a plausible cause that was never supplied.

This toolkit puts structure around the drafting stage. You separate validated figures, known evidence, assumptions and unresolved unknowns before AI writes the narrative, then use finance-specific checks before sign-off.

## How it works

1. Add figures you have already validated.
2. Record what is actually known about the material movements and what remains unresolved.
3. Set the wording and terminology you want the AI to follow.
4. Use the reusable finance-specific instruction with your approved general-purpose AI assistant.
5. Review the draft using the verification checklist and apply your professional judgement before sign-off.

## Who it is for

Finance professionals who already prepare management accounts and want to reduce the writing and checking burden of turning validated figures and known business context into concise management commentary.

The user should already be able to validate the accounts and exercise professional judgement. This is a drafting and quality-control toolkit, not an accounting course or outsourced finance service.

## Who it is not for

It is not designed to calculate or reconcile accounts, perform the underlying variance investigation, make accounting judgements, or provide professional sign-off. It does not include calls, review of a customer's real management accounts, bespoke prompt engineering or software implementation.

## Key reassurance

The workflow is deliberately designed to keep professional judgement with finance. AI is used as a drafting and challenge aid. The finance professional remains responsible for the underlying numbers, evidence, accounting treatment, materiality and final sign-off.

## Safe data note

## FAQ

### Does this calculate my management accounts?
No. Start with figures you have already validated. The toolkit helps with the narrative and verification stage.

### Which AI tool do I need?
A general-purpose AI assistant capable of following detailed text instructions. The toolkit does not require or certify a particular provider. Use only a service approved by your organisation for the information you intend to provide.

### Can I paste confidential company accounts into AI?
Only if your organisation has approved the AI service and the proposed use of that data. If you are unsure, anonymise or fictionalise the information and remove identifiers.

### Will AI write the final commentary for me?
It can produce a first draft, but you remain responsible for checking the figures, evidence, wording, accounting judgement and final sign-off.

### Is this accounting advice?
No. It is a self-service drafting and quality-control toolkit for finance professionals.

### Is support included?
The product contains workflow troubleshooting. It does not include calls, review of real management accounts, bespoke prompt engineering or accounting advice.
