## Stop conditions

Stop or reposition the paid workflow if:

- independent users do not experience a net time saving;
- critical factual or analytical failures persist;
- a simple free instruction performs equally well in clean independent testing;
- users value the output but will not pay for the reusable toolkit;
- support requirements turn the product into a service.
