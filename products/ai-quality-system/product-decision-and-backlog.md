## Stop conditions

Stop or reposition the paid workflow if:

- independent users do not experience a net time saving;
- critical factual or analytical failures persist after the controls are applied;
- the free one-sentence uncertainty instruction performs equally well in realistic independent use;
- customers value the drafting but will not pay for the reusable toolkit;
- support requirements turn the product into a service.
