# England Bereavement Guide — authoritative practical-process research foundation

**Date:** 26 September 2026
**Status:** GREEN research deliverable — QA PASS
**Scope:** England-specific practical administration foundation for Part One: *What you need to do*. This is source research, not legal advice or final customer copy.

## Product intent

The guide must reduce cognitive load after a death. It should distinguish what is urgent, what can wait, what government services cover automatically, and what the bereaved person still has to contact themselves. It must not present grief administration as a single neat timetable when coroner, overseas-death and other circumstances alter the process.

## Verified practical sequence

### 1. Before registration
Current GOV.UK guidance says the doctor confirms the death and the medical examiner checks the cause. The medical examiner's office then confirms when registration can proceed.

### 2. Register the death
In England, GOV.UK says to register within 5 days of the medical examiner's confirmation, including weekends and bank holidays. If more time is needed, the register office should be told immediately.

After registration, the registrar provides the certificate for burial or cremation, often called the green form, for the funeral director, crematorium or burial authority.

**Product implication:** do not use the outdated/simple wording "register within five days of the death". The trigger is the medical examiner's confirmation under current guidance.

### 3. Tell Us Once
Tell Us Once can notify many government organisations in one action. A registrar may complete it with the user or provide a unique reference number. GOV.UK says the service must be used within 28 days of receiving that reference.

It can notify HMRC, DWP, Passport Office, DVLA, the local council, Veterans UK and specified public-sector pension schemes, subject to the person's circumstances.

**Important limitation:** it does not replace notifying private/commercial organisations.

### 4. Organisations still needing separate contact
GOV.UK specifically identifies banks, mortgage providers, insurers, utilities/contract providers, landlords/housing associations and personal/workplace pensions outside the covered public schemes. Depending on circumstances, other separate actions can include property records, vehicles, business taxes and student loans.

**Product implication:** use two visibly separate checklists:
- Government notifications handled/partly handled by Tell Us Once
- Organisations you may still need to contact yourself

This prevents duplicated effort and false reassurance.

### 5. Coroner route
Where a death is reported to a coroner, an interim death certificate can be used as proof while an inquest is pending. GOV.UK states that an interim or final certificate can be used for probate and Tell Us Once. Funeral timing/process can differ.

**Product implication:** add a clearly signposted "If the coroner is involved" route rather than forcing these users through the ordinary sequence.

### 6. Death abroad
A death abroad must be registered with local authorities in the country of death. GOV.UK has a separate route for England/Wales residents and explains when Tell Us Once can still be used.

**Product implication:** include a signpost, not an overlong attempt to cover every country's process.

## Support / safety foundation for Part Two

The NHS service directory lists the Bereavement Advice Centre as a nationwide source of practical bereavement information and signposting, including registration/coroner questions, funerals, benefits, probate and estate administration.

Samaritans provides free emotional support 24 hours a day, 365 days a year on **116 123**. This belongs in a clearly visible urgent-support box in the emotional-support half of the guide, while making clear that Samaritans is broader crisis/emotional support rather than a substitute for specialist bereavement services.

## CREATE -> CRITIQUE -> FIX -> RE-CHECK

### First-pass defects identified
- Risk of repeating the common but imprecise "five days after death" registration wording.
- Risk of implying Tell Us Once closes every account.
- Risk of designing one linear checklist that fails coroner cases.
- Risk of mixing emotional crisis support into the administrative checklist without context.

### Fixes
- Anchored the five-day requirement to medical-examiner confirmation.
- Split government notification from private/commercial notification.
- Added explicit coroner and overseas branches.
- Kept crisis/emotional support as a Part Two foundation while preserving a visible safety route.

### Re-check
PASS for research-foundation use. Final customer copy still requires source links/current-date verification at production time and further specialist-support research for child loss, suicide bereavement, Armed Forces bereavement and former carers.
