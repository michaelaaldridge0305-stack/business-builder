# Search and Discoverability Research

**Research date:** 9 September 2026
**Stage:** SEARCH / DISCOVERABILITY RESEARCH

## Finding

Search intent clearly exists, but broad web intent is heavily served by authoritative and free resources. Etsy can capture purchase intent for printable/downloadable organisation, while Pinterest is likely better suited to problem/solution discovery than to procedural legal keywords.

This research establishes route-to-market plausibility, not keyword-volume proof. Exact platform search volumes were not available from public sources in this run and should not be invented.

## High-intent language observed in live results

### Broad problem intent

- what to do when someone dies
- what to do after someone dies
- what to do when someone dies checklist
- who to notify when someone dies

These terms surface GOV.UK, Marie Curie and free checklist tools prominently. They are useful content/Pinterest themes but commercially difficult as pure web-search product terms.

### Administrative / buyer intent

- bereavement checklist UK
- bereavement organiser
- after death admin
- executor checklist UK
- probate tracker / probate organiser
- funeral planner UK

Etsy products use combinations of these phrases in titles. This indicates marketplace relevance, although title usage is not proof of strong volume.

## Etsy fit

The surfaced Etsy market includes a UK checklist at US$12.42 and a broader UK funeral/bereavement/probate organiser at US$16.79. The first listing had 24 favourites and five item reviews at research date. This is small-scale but real purchase-intent evidence.

A future listing should not rely on "bereavement planner" alone because that can imply emotional journaling or funeral planning. The product's functional language should emphasise **admin**, **checklist/system**, **who to notify**, **executor**, **documents**, and **tracking**, subject to actual Etsy keyword validation at listing stage.

## Pinterest fit

Pinterest should not be treated as a place to publish dense procedural instructions. Better Pin angles would create recognition and lead to an organisational solution, for example:

- "You used Tell Us Once. Who still needs to be contacted?"
- "The admin after a death is not one checklist. It is dozens of conversations to keep track of."
- "What needs doing now, what can wait, and what is still outstanding?"
- "A calmer way to organise bereavement paperwork"
- "Executor admin: one place for contacts, documents, reference numbers and follow-ups"

These are concept directions only, not approved customer-facing assets.

## Discovery risk

The category is vulnerable to free-resource substitution. A Pin promising only a checklist may send users to GOV.UK or a charity instead of creating purchase desire. Marketing needs to make the **organisational layer** visible: tracking, follow-ups, documents, outstanding actions and a staged workflow.

## Evidence gaps

Before a final Etsy listing is created, use available Etsy seller/search data to validate actual query terms and competition. If Pinterest analytics later becomes available, compare outbound CTR for problem-led versus executor/probate-led creative. These are optimisation steps, not blockers to deciding whether the product concept deserves build.
