# Proposed Product Specification

**Status:** PROPOSED, NOT AUTHORISED FOR BUILD UNTIL OWNER APPROVES VALIDATION GATE
**Based on validation report dated:** 9 September 2026

## Product concept

A calm, practical **England and Wales bereavement administration organiser** that helps a family member, executor or administrator keep control of practical tasks after an adult death.

It is an administration system, not an ebook, grief journal, legal guide or notification service.

## Job to be done

When I am dealing with practical administration after someone dies, help me see what needs attention, collect the information I keep being asked for, record who I have contacted and what they need, and see what is still outstanding so I do not have to hold it all in my head.

## Scope

### Included

- England and Wales core route
- first-days / next-weeks / later staging
- Tell Us Once plus private-organisation gap
- document and information organisation
- notification/contact tracking
- follow-up tracking
- asset/debt discovery organisation
- executor/estate progress signposting
- authoritative links
- bereavement support signposting

### Excluded / signposted

- Scotland and Northern Ireland procedure
- personalised probate/legal/tax advice
- complex estate instructions
- full funeral planning workbook
- grief therapy/journaling
- sending notifications on the user's behalf
- storing passwords

## Page architecture

1. Cover
2. How to use this organiser + scope
3. Your next steps: Now / Soon / Later
4. If the coroner is involved / death abroad: route-to-official-guidance signpost
5. Key information about the person who died
6. Key people and responsibilities
7. Important documents: what exists and where it is
8. Death certificates and document-copy tracker
9. First actions dashboard
10. Tell Us Once: what it helps with / what still remains
11. Who else may need to be told: category inventory
12-14. Contact & follow-up log sheets
15. Accounts, assets and income sources discovery
16. Debts, bills and regular payments discovery
17. Property, vehicle and household administration
18. Digital accounts, memberships and subscriptions
19. Estate administration progress map
20. Estate values / information-request tracker (organisation only)
21. Outstanding actions review
22. Notes / questions for professionals
23. Official sources and support
24. Privacy, storage and version information

Repeated log sheets may bring final useful length to approximately 24-28 pages.

## Functional design rules

- Every page must answer a real administrative need.
- Use checkboxes/status fields only where they reduce cognitive load.
- Prefer short prompts and tables over explanatory paragraphs.
- Visually distinguish "do/record this" from "check official guidance".
- Provide status choices such as Not started / Waiting / Follow up / Done where useful.
- Contact log should include: organisation; purpose; date; method; person/team; information/documents requested; information/documents sent; reference number; outcome; next action; follow-up date; status.
- Do not request passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
- Provide a reminder that completed files may contain sensitive personal/financial information and should be stored securely.

## Brand and visual direction

Ikhaya Designs GB only. Clean greens/neutrals, restrained eucalyptus/botanical elements, generous spacing, practical hierarchy. The product should feel calm and capable rather than sombre, sentimental or clinical.

The Etsy hero image, if later authorised, must show the working system rather than mainly the cover.

## Accuracy architecture

- Version/date on product.
- Authoritative source page with GOV.UK links.
- Procedural sections kept concise to reduce staleness.
- Volatile fees/thresholds avoided unless essential.
- Clear England-and-Wales scope.
- Annual review minimum, plus event-driven review after material government process changes.

## Commercial test

Provisional price test range from validation: £9-£15. Final price is not approved by this specification.

The product should be abandoned or substantially revised if build QA cannot demonstrate paid value beyond free checklists, particularly the contact/follow-up/document/outstanding-action control layer.
