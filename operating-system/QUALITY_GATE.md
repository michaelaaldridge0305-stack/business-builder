# Business Builder Quality Gate v1.0

## Purpose

This is the mandatory quality-control and publishing gate for substantive Business Builder work. The objective is to prevent weak, incomplete or off-brand work from reaching Michaela as if it were finished.

The required workflow is:

**BRIEF → BUILD → SPECIALIST QA → CORRECT → FINAL QA → APPROVAL/PUBLISH → MEASURE**

Michaela should normally enter after FINAL QA, not between BUILD and QA.

## Status language

Do not use vague completion language such as “done” or “finished” without a QA status.

Use only:

- DRAFT
- QA FAILED
- CORRECTING
- QA PASSED
- AWAITING MICHAELA APPROVAL
- PUBLISHED / SCHEDULED

If a check cannot genuinely be performed, mark it **NOT VERIFIED**. Never convert an unverified check into PASS.

## Universal rules

Before any substantive commercial asset is presented as QA PASSED, verify all relevant points below:

- The work solves the intended problem and is commercially credible.
- The output matches the current brand, positioning and audience.
- Claims, numbers, features, file formats and product descriptions are accurate.
- No slogan, testimonial, review, credential, feature, result or other claim has been invented.
- Spelling, grammar, terminology, links and factual details have been checked.
- Layout, hierarchy, spacing, alignment, cropping and legibility are professional.
- Any customer-facing visual shows genuine value rather than relying only on a cover or decorative presentation.
- Any material issue identified during QA is corrected before the work is shown to Michaela, unless correction genuinely requires her decision.
- If conflicting standards or source files exist, resolve the conflict before editing. Do not blindly apply isolated feedback without checking connected consequences.

## Ikhaya Designs GB product gate

A product should not progress to marketing unless it passes the following:

### Problem and transformation

- Solves a real and specific customer problem.
- Fits Ikhaya Designs GB’s practical “chaos to calm” positioning.
- Reduces overwhelm, improves organisation, or creates clarity, confidence or control.
- Delivers a meaningful transformation or useful customer journey rather than generic printable clutter.

### Product quality

- Functions correctly.
- Instructions are sufficient for the intended customer.
- Pages, sections, prompts or features have a clear reason to exist.
- There is no filler, repetition without purpose, or decorative content pretending to be value.
- The customer journey is coherent where relevant: problem → clarity → action → repetition → progress.

### Commercial viability

- The product is worth promoting now relative to existing priorities.
- Existing products and near-complete work have been considered before creating something new.
- The likely demand, speed to revenue, effort, reuse, scalability and dependence on Michaela justify the work.

### Brand

- Clean, sophisticated and practical.
- Greens and neutrals unless a product-specific palette has been explicitly approved.
- Restrained eucalyptus/botanical elements where appropriate.
- No unnecessary pink.
- Correct Ikhaya Designs GB logo where a logo is required.
- Professional spacing and hierarchy.
- Calm, but not vague, bland or overly delicate.

## Etsy gate

Every Etsy listing or major listing refresh must begin with a listing brief that defines:

1. Exact product.
2. Customer.
3. Problem being solved.
4. Search intent.
5. Product format and what the buyer receives.
6. Primary transformation or payoff.
7. Main objections or uncertainties.
8. Hero-image job.
9. Supporting image sequence.
10. Any trust, compatibility or access information that must be visible.

Then review the listing across the five required pillars:

### Visibility

- Title and keywords reflect genuine buyer search intent.
- Hero image can compete in Etsy search and category thumbnails.
- First image communicates the product quickly at small size.
- Important wording is readable on mobile.

### Clarity

- Within seconds, the buyer can understand what the product is.
- The intended customer is obvious.
- The buyer can see what they receive.
- The product format, compatibility and access method are clear.
- Image sequence and copy do not contradict each other.

### Desire

- The listing sells the useful outcome, not only the file type.
- Images reveal meaningful product pages, features, systems or customer journey.
- The offer visibly feels more useful than a generic printable or AI-generated filler product.
- The product’s strongest differentiators are demonstrated, not merely asserted.

### Trust

- Claims are supportable.
- Any credentials are accurate.
- No fake reviews, testimonials, results or urgency.
- Relevant reassurance is included where uncertainty could block purchase.
- Product screenshots/mockups accurately represent what is delivered.

### Ease

- What is included is explicit.
- File types, compatibility, printing, access and usage are explained where relevant.
- Important limitations are disclosed.
- Common objections are answered without overwhelming the listing.
- The buyer should know what happens after purchase.

### Etsy visual sequence

Use a deliberate sequence rather than ten decorative variants. As a default, consider:

1. Hero: product + problem + value.
2. Transformation / key outcome.
3. Meaningful contents.
4. How it works / customer journey.
5. Detailed feature or page previews.
6. What is included.
7. Format / compatibility / file information.
8. Access / printing / setup.
9. Objection handling / FAQ / trust.
10. Related product or bundle only if genuinely useful.

Before approval, test the hero image at Etsy thumbnail crop and mobile scale.

## Pinterest gate

Treat Pinterest as a commercial search/discovery channel, not a vanity-content channel.

Before design, define:

1. Etsy product being promoted.
2. Person and problem.
3. Likely Pinterest search intent.
4. Primary keyword.
5. Secondary keyword cluster.
6. Promise or transformation.
7. Creative format.
8. Click trigger.
9. Headline.
10. Exact Etsy destination.
11. Test variable.

Every Pin must then pass:

### Search

- Targets a real and relevant search intent.
- Wording reflects how the customer describes the problem or outcome.
- Keywords are natural and honest rather than stuffed.

### Product and value

- The viewer can identify what the resource helps with.
- The visual shows meaningful value rather than only a cover.
- The promised outcome is credible and supported by the product.

### Mobile and brand

- Main message is readable immediately on a phone.
- Strong hierarchy and contrast.
- One obvious focal point.
- Product is not hidden by styling.
- Matches Ikhaya Designs GB’s calm, practical brand.
- Correct branding is used where appropriate.

### Click trigger

The Pin must provide at least one genuine reason to click through, using one or more of:

- Clarity.
- Payoff.
- Curiosity.
- Trust / ease.

A beautiful or calming visual can be a valid creative format, but aesthetic performance alone does not make it a sales Pin. If the commercial goal is Etsy traffic, there must be a deliberate bridge from the visual to a relevant problem, outcome, product or next step.

### Destination

- Links directly to the exact relevant Etsy listing unless another destination has been deliberately approved.
- Pin promise matches the listing immediately.
- Etsy hero image and listing copy provide continuity with the Pin.

### Accuracy

- All claims are supportable.
- Product format is clear.
- Sensitive health, finance, security or productivity statements are appropriately cautious.
- The Pin does not imply a product replaces professional advice where it does not.

### Pin-family rule

Do not use one product = one Pin.

For priority products, create distinct discovery routes such as:

- problem recognition;
- outcome;
- search clarity;
- method education;
- product walkthrough;
- before/after;
- objection handling;
- trust/ease;
- curiosity;
- collection/pairing where genuinely useful.

Do not publish repeated near-identical designs simply to increase volume.

## Final QA gate

Before an item is marked QA PASSED, run one final independent pass covering:

- Problem solved.
- Commercial objective.
- Brand consistency.
- Accuracy.
- Functionality where applicable.
- Visual quality.
- Mobile/crop behaviour where applicable.
- Copy quality.
- Link/destination accuracy.
- Consistency across product, listing and promotional asset.
- No unsupported claims.
- No unresolved material issue hidden by presentation polish.

If the reviewer finds material issues, change the status to QA FAILED or CORRECTING and fix them before presenting the asset.

## QA receipt

For substantial work, provide a compact receipt in this format:

**BUSINESS BUILDER QA: PASSED / FAILED / PARTIAL**

- Product / objective: PASS / FAIL / NOT VERIFIED
- Specialist standard: PASS / FAIL / NOT VERIFIED
- Brand: PASS / FAIL / NOT VERIFIED
- Accuracy / functionality: PASS / FAIL / NOT VERIFIED
- Mobile / crop / technical presentation: PASS / FAIL / NOT APPLICABLE / NOT VERIFIED
- Issues found during QA: [number]
- Issues corrected before delivery: [number]
- Outstanding material issues: [number + concise description]

A high numerical score must never override a hard fail.

## Approval rules

- Do not publish, schedule, merge, send or make another external change requiring Michaela’s approval unless she has explicitly authorised that action.
- Once she has clearly approved a defined set of changes, execute them without repeatedly reopening settled decisions.
- If a platform/tool prevents completion, report exactly what is blocked and what remains completed. Do not imply completion.

## Measurement loop

After publication, evaluate commercial performance against the objective rather than vanity metrics.

For Pinterest → Etsy work, prioritise:

1. Orders and revenue where measurable.
2. Etsy visits from Pinterest.
3. Outbound clicks.
4. Outbound CTR.
5. Favourites or purchase-intent signals.
6. Saves.
7. Impressions.

When a creative performs, identify the underlying winning principle and build controlled variations rather than simply duplicating the asset.

## Source-of-truth rule

Current product specifications, approved brand decisions, Etsy operating rules, Pinterest strategy files and other explicit Business Builder standards override recalled assumptions.

Always inspect the relevant current source material before major commercial work. If a source is unavailable, state that it was not verified rather than relying on memory alone.
