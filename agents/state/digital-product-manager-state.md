### Ikhaya Automations

Ikhaya Automations is primarily an education and audience-building business. Its purpose is to make practical AI and automation understandable and useful for small businesses, especially organisations that want to do more with tools and budgets they already have.

The immediate paid-product push remains paused. Do not restart `Numbers to Management Narrative` or begin another Ikhaya Automations paid product unless Michaela explicitly reauthorises that stream.

### Ikhaya Designs GB

Ikhaya Designs GB creates practical digital tools that help people move **from chaos to calm**. It is not a planner shop. A planner may be one product format, but the business should be positioned around practical tools that reduce overwhelm, improve organisation and make difficult or busy parts of life easier to manage.

The primary sales channel is Etsy. Pinterest is a traffic and discovery channel whose purpose is to drive qualified outbound clicks to relevant Etsy listings. Instagram is a supporting awareness and product-promotion channel.

### Priority 2: execute the Pinterest-to-Etsy strategy

Pinterest should be treated primarily as a visual search and discovery engine rather than a conventional social network.

The commercial funnel is:

`Pinterest search/discovery -> compelling Pin -> outbound click -> relevant Etsy listing -> purchase`

Optimise primarily for:

1. qualified Pinterest impressions;
2. outbound clicks;
3. outbound click-through rate;
4. Etsy listing visits;
5. Etsy favourites and purchase intent;
6. Etsy orders and revenue.

Do not optimise for followers, saves or impressions as vanity metrics when they do not contribute to qualified traffic or sales.

For each priority Etsy product, develop multiple Pin angles rather than repeating one creative. Strong categories include:

- problem-aware pins;
- solution-led pins;
- outcome-led pins;
- search-led educational pins;
- framework or concept-led pins where genuinely relevant;
- product demonstration pins;
- before/after or chaos-to-calm transformations;
- buyer-intent keyword pins.

Every Pin should have a clear match between the search intent, Pin promise and destination Etsy listing. Avoid clickbait or broad traffic that is unlikely to convert.

Pinterest work may include keyword research, creative concepts, copy, pin specifications, board strategy, scheduling recommendations and performance analysis. External posting or scheduling must follow the applicable approval/access rules.

## Current Ikhaya Designs GB operating lessons

### Brand and positioning

- Core promise: practical tools that help people move from chaos to calm.
- Do not describe the business as primarily a planner shop.
- Prefer clean, calm, practical, professional design.
- Avoid pink unless specifically requested.
- Use the correct Ikhaya Designs GB logo on final assets where appropriate.
- Existing successful green/neutral styling and restrained botanical/eucalyptus elements may be used where they fit the product.
- Customer-facing copy should sound human, useful and specific rather than AI-generated.
- Do not use em dashes in customer-facing copy.

### Etsy listing principle

A listing image set should do much of the selling without requiring the buyer to read a long description first. Images should progressively answer:

- What is this?
- Is it for me?
- What problem does it solve?
- What exactly do I receive?
- Why is this different or better suited to my need?
- Can I trust it?
- How do I use or access it?

The first image must work at Etsy thumbnail size and on mobile. Do not approve aesthetically pleasant but commercially weak imagery.

### Pinterest principle

A Pin is not simply a resized Etsy listing image. It needs its own search intent, visual hook and promise while remaining tightly aligned with the destination product.

Create enough creative and keyword variation to learn which customer problems and messages produce outbound clicks.
