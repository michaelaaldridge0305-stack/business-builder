## Objective

Redesign Ikhaya Designs GB's Etsy listing imagery so the shop is visually competitive in Etsy search and the full image sequence performs as a sales page.

The owner’s central concern is that current Etsy images are not good enough compared with competitors. The working commercial premise is simple: before a buyer can purchase, the listing imagery must earn the click and communicate value.

This is now a higher-priority conversion workstream than further theoretical description/SEO/pricing optimisation unless new evidence changes that priority.

## Owner direction

The visual identity should communicate:

- class
- elegance
- sophistication
- organisation
- calm

Ikhaya Designs GB's broader promise remains **From Chaos to Calm**.

Do not make the brand generic, overly decorative, tacky, cluttered, or visually noisy. Do not copy competitor aesthetics. Study competitors for conversion mechanics, hierarchy, product merchandising and perceived value, then express those lessons in Ikhaya's own visual language.

## Core conversion test

For every hero image ask:

> If this Ikhaya listing appeared directly beside the strongest competitors in Etsy search, would the Ikhaya thumbnail earn the click?

A design that looks attractive at full size but becomes weak, vague or illegible at Etsy search-thumbnail/mobile size has failed.

The product should dominate the image. Decoration should support it.

## Lessons already identified from competitor review

Do not treat these as a substitute for inspecting the actual competitor listings again when redesigning a specific product.

1. Stronger digital-product listings sell the **system/outcome**, not merely the file.
2. Hero images need immediate product recognition and strong hierarchy.
3. Genuine product proof should be large enough to inspect, not tiny screenshots floating inside decorative mock-ups.
4. A full image set should reveal different benefits/jobs/parts of the product rather than repeat similar overview images.
5. Image sequences should increase perceived product depth and tangibility.
6. Useful merchandising sequence: **STOP → UNDERSTAND → WANT → PROVE → REASSURE → BUY**.
7. Search-thumbnail performance is a mandatory QA check.
8. The buyer should understand both what the product is and why it helps within seconds.

## Real-product fidelity rule

This is non-negotiable.

Where an Etsy image purports to show the actual product, use genuine Ikhaya product pages/screenshots. Do not use AI-generated spreadsheet, planner, Notion or dashboard interfaces as product proof.

AI-generated or stock-style surroundings may be used only where they do not misrepresent what the customer receives.

## Required redesign approach

Start again from composition rather than simply editing text sizes.

For each product:

1. Inspect the live/current Ikhaya listing imagery.
2. Inspect several strong relevant Etsy competitors in the actual buyer environment.
3. Identify conversion mechanics, not merely colours/style.
4. Inspect genuine Ikhaya product assets.
5. Design the hero around immediate recognition + outcome + large authentic product proof.
6. Test at Etsy search-thumbnail/mobile size.
7. Critique against the competitor set.
8. Fix weaknesses.
9. Re-check spelling, contrast, hierarchy, cropping, fidelity and perceived value.
10. Only after PASS present it as an approval-stage design.
11. After hero approval, build the rest of the image sequence as a coherent visual sales journey.

## Suggested full-listing image logic

Adapt to each product rather than applying mechanically:

1. **Hero / search thumbnail** — product recognition + strongest outcome + genuine proof
2. **Problem / buyer recognition** — why the product exists
3. **What you get** — breadth and substance
4. **Key outcome/feature** — genuine product close-up
5. **Second major outcome/feature**
6. **How it works** — reduce complexity/purchase anxiety
7. **Who it is for / use case**
8. **Detailed product proof** — inspectable genuine pages/screens
9. **Delivery / getting started / compatibility**, only where factually verified
10. **Final value reinforcement / CTA**

Do not force ten images if fewer tell the story better.

## Pinterest completion-state rule

A separate operational issue was recently fixed. Do not confuse Pinterest specifications with finished assets.

Pinterest states are:

**CONCEPT → SPEC READY → ASSET READY → READY TO SCHEDULE → SCHEDULED → PUBLISHED**

A Pin is not READY TO SCHEDULE until an actual finished image exists and has passed visual/mobile/product-fidelity QA, with title, description, exact Etsy link, board and publishing slot complete.

This same discipline should inform Etsy image work: a written creative specification is not a finished design.
